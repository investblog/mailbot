FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no payment, no limits to sign up. Open the bot and use it.

What happens after 24 hours?

The address expires and stops receiving mail. Messages already delivered stay in your Telegram chat.

Do you store my emails?

No. Mail is parsed at the edge and forwarded to your chat. We don't keep the contents on our side.

Can I send email from the address?

No — it's receive-only, built for catching one-time codes and confirmation links.

Should I use it for important accounts?

No. It's a throwaway address: temporary by design. Use your real inbox for anything you need to keep.

What if the code isn't detected?

The full email still arrives in your chat — you'll see the code in the body. We only highlight it when we're confident.
